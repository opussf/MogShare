# MogShare  or MogScan

This addon saves mogs.
You can review and rate them for later.

## Why

Have you ever seen a player with a nice mog, and they ran off before you could inspect them, and get a picture of their mog set?

## Solution

This scans players, and captures their Custom Mog link for their current look.
It saves it for later when you can take a look in the dressing room.

## Game

This also includes a 'mog war' game.
Where you are shown 2 mogs, and you pick the one you like best.
